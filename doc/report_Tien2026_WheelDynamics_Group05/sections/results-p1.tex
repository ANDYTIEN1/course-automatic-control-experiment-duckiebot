\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
\subsection{P1: Low-duty response and dead zone}
For the right wheel of R5 (motor m10), the rest recordings gave a motion threshold of $\OmegaMin$ rad/s. Only $\RestBeyond$ of $\RestFrames$ frames recorded at rest exceeded this threshold in absolute speed (Fig.~\ref{fig:g5-rest-noise}).

On the increasing positive-duty sweep, the measured speed at $u=0.20$ was $\SpeedAtTwo$ rad/s (mean $\pm$ standard deviation). Its mean lay below the motion threshold, whereas the gain fitted to the step from rest to $u=0.6$ predicted $\PredTwo$ rad/s. The first sampled level with detectable rotation was $u=\StartDuty$, where the speed reached $\SpeedAtStart$ rad/s (Fig.~\ref{fig:g5-small-duty}). Thus a nonzero command could leave the wheel at rest, contrary to P1.
